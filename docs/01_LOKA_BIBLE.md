# LOKA BIBLE

# 1. Vision

To become Indonesia's most trusted AI Train Travel Companion, empowering every train traveler to plan smarter, travel with confidence, and enjoy a stress-free journey.

LOKA aims to become the first application people open before booking a train ticket and the last application they need before departure.

Our long-term vision is to transform train travel from a simple transportation activity into a well-planned and enjoyable travel experience powered by artificial intelligence.

---

# 2. Mission

LOKA exists to simplify every stage of train travel.

We help travelers:

- Plan their journey intelligently.
- Estimate travel expenses accurately.
- Organize itineraries efficiently.
- Receive timely travel reminders.
- Get personalized recommendations from RAILA AI.
- Travel with greater confidence and peace of mind.

LOKA complements official railway booking platforms by focusing on planning, organization, and intelligent travel assistance.

---

# 3. Product Overview

LOKA is an AI-powered Train Travel Companion designed specifically for train travelers.

Unlike ticket booking applications, LOKA focuses on helping users prepare for their journey before departure, during the trip, and after arriving at their destination.

LOKA combines artificial intelligence, budget estimation, itinerary planning, reminders, and travel guidance into a single experience.

Our AI assistant, RAILA, acts as a personal travel consultant, helping users make better travel decisions while reducing uncertainty and travel-related stress.

---

# 4. Problem Statement

Although purchasing train tickets has become easier through official booking platforms, travelers still face many challenges before departure.

Common problems include:

- Difficulty estimating the total travel budget.
- Uncertainty about additional expenses during the trip.
- Lack of personalized travel planning.
- Forgetting important departure schedules.
- Difficulty organizing travel itineraries.
- Limited travel guidance after purchasing tickets.
- Travelers often search for information across multiple applications.

These fragmented experiences create unnecessary stress and reduce overall travel confidence.

LOKA solves these problems by becoming a single AI-powered travel companion dedicated to train journeys.
